Gentle Steps — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Gentle Steps ("the App") is a step counter that uses your phone's built-in motion sensors. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App uses and how it is handled.

Summary

  • All step and settings data stays on your device only.
  • We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data.
  • No GPS or location tracking.
  • No account registration required.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally:

  • Daily step counts and step history
  • Daily step goal and reminder preferences
  • Theme (light/dark) and language settings
  • Onboarding completion status

We do not upload this data to our servers because we do not operate backend servers for the App.

Motion Sensors & Permissions

The App uses motion sensors (such as the accelerometer or step counter) to count steps. On Android, the App may request Physical activity permission so step counting can work.

The App does not use GPS and does not track your location.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device (for example, walk reminders or goal alerts). Notification content is generated on your device. We do not send your data to third parties for notifications.

Fonts

The App may download font files from Google Fonts on first use. That request goes to Google's servers; we do not send your personal data as part of that request. See Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Data Retention & Deletion

All App data remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear the App's storage in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the Google Play store listing for Gentle Steps.
