Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Water Reminder ("the App") helps you track daily water intake. Your privacy is important to us.

Information We Collect

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Water intake logs (amount and time)
  • Daily hydration goal
  • Reminder preferences (enabled/disabled and interval)
  • Theme preference (light/dark)
  • Onboarding completion status

We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on external servers.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device. No data is sent to third parties for this feature.

Fonts

The App may download font files from Google Fonts on first use. This involves a network request to Google; no personal data is shared.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

Data Retention & Deletion

All data remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear the App's storage.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the app store listing.
